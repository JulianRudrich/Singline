Free and open source · Windows, macOS and Linux

Made for listening.

Everything you need to follow a song, and nothing that gets in the way.

Word by word

Every word lights up as it is sung, from real word timing where the lyrics have it.

Always on top

A small window above every app, on every desktop. It never takes the focus and can let clicks through.

A mini-player

Previous, play and next, a seek bar and chorus markers appear when you move the pointer over it.

Music in your browser

YouTube Music, YouTube and other web players, even in a background tab, with the Singline extension.

Sing to find

Sing a few words: Singline recognises them on your computer, finds the song and follows you from the line you sang.

Karaoke

Fill a second screen or a TV with the lyrics, word by word, with a countdown before every line.

Your own lyrics

Import an LRC file or set each line's timestamp with a key press while the song plays.

In your terminal

The line being sung in the footer of pi, in your tmux status bar or in a terminal of its own, for coding with music.

Your music

Listening time, streaks, a listening clock and your top songs and artists, kept on your device.

True Shuffle

Connect Spotify for an honest random order in which nothing repeats until everything has played.

Works offline

Lyrics are cached on your device, so songs you have heard before work without a connection.

Private

No account, no tracking. Only the song's details leave your computer, to find its lyrics, and Sing to find never sends audio.

Yours to arrange.

One line, a focus on the current line, or the lines around it. Font size, transparency and size are up to you.

Karaoke on the big screen.

Put the lyrics on a second screen or a TV: large, white on black, word by word, with a countdown before every line.

Works with your player.

Singline reads what your music app is playing. It never needs your password and never starts a player by itself.
